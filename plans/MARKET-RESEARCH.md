# Silex Digital - Bulgarian Market Research & Strategic Positioning

**Date:** March 2026  
**Purpose:** Market analysis for platform redesign targeting Bulgarian SME automation market

---

## Executive Summary

The Bulgarian digital market presents a **significant opportunity** for an automation-focused agency. While most competitors focus on basic web development, the automation + AI agents niche remains underserved for Bulgarian SMEs. The key differentiator: **showing, not telling** — interactive demos that let prospects experience value before the sales call.

## 2. Target Audience Profile

### 2.1 Primary: Bulgarian SMEs (10-100 employees)

**Characteristics:**

- Revenue: 500K - 10M BGN annually
- Already using: Excel, outdated CRM, manual processes
- Pain points:
  - Repetitive tasks consuming 15-20 hours/week
  - Data scattered across 5+ disconnected tools
  - Growing but can't afford more staff
  - Competitors automating faster

**Decision makers:**

- Owner/CEO (70% of decisions)
- Operations Manager (20%)
- IT Manager (10% - rare)

### 2.2 Secondary: Freelancers & Small Agencies

- Need automation for their own business
- Potential referral partners
- Lower budget, faster decisions

### 2.3 Tertiary: International Companies

- Looking for Bulgarian development team
- Cost arbitrage opportunity
- English-speaking

## 8. Technical Recommendations

### 8.1 Current Stack Assessment

**✅ Keep:**

- Next.js 15 (excellent choice)
- TypeScript (non-negotiable)
- Tailwind CSS 4 (modern, fast)
- Supabase (good for this scale)
- Framer Motion (animation quality)

**🔧 Optimize:**

- Reduce Three.js hero (performance cost vs. benefit)
- Consider alternatives for 3D: CSS animations, Lottie
- Add error tracking (Sentry)

**➕ Add:**

- Analytics (Plausible for privacy-focused BG market)
- Heatmaps (Hotjar or Plausible)
- A/B testing capability
- Multi-language support (i18n)
