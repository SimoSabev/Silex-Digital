## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A unified, modern automation-first platform that transforms how small and medium businesses in Bulgaria capture and convert clients. The platform operates on three integrated layers:

1. **AUTOMATION LAYER** - The foundation that makes everything work smarter
2. **CREATION LAYER - CODE** - For businesses building from scratch or needing custom solutions
3. **CREATION LAYER - CMS** - For businesses wanting modern, managed solutions without coding

Whether a business already has a website/app or is starting fresh, they get ONE integrated solution that automates client capture, responds intelligently, and generates conversions—with flexibility to choose their tech stack.

**Core Promise:** "Automation that turns every client interaction into an opportunity"

**Market Positioning:** The modern way Bulgarian businesses scale—no manual work, no missed clients, no outdated technology

- Colors: Brand colors (blues, greens, whites)

- All animations have captions
- "Reduce motion" mode for sensitive users
- Text alternative to every animation
